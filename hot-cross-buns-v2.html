Hot Cross Buns V2 Trumpet practice · watch the valves, play the note

Play along with the valve highway

Notes travel toward the three valve buttons, stacked like your trumpet: valve 3 on top (farthest from the mouthpiece), valve 1 at the bottom. Each note lights up the valves to press. A purple bar means open, with no valves down. Prefer the V1 highway? Switch it under Valve layout in Settings.

  1. Press Start playing and allow the microphone.
  2. Hold your trumpet ready during the 4-click count-in.
  3. Play each note as it reaches the valve buttons. The mic checks your pitch.

New to the song? Turn on Wait for me. The song stops at each note until you play it.

This page needs to be opened in Chrome, Edge or Safari for the microphone to work.
